## What was wrong

PV-22 found the system `vendomat` at 0.4.4. This session found three more facts:

- The host delta already exists. `machines/server.nix` imports `inputs.vendomat.nixosModules.default`, which installs `packages.<system>.vendomat` and links `/share/vendomat`. No new host delta is needed (`DEL-007` holds).
- The `nix-meta` pin (`dc070d4`) builds `vendomat-0.4.6`. It lacks the store step, so a switch to `main` would not bring `path` or the new `sync` flags.
- The store step kept the version at 0.4.6. A 0.4.6 built before and after the step could not be told apart by name.
