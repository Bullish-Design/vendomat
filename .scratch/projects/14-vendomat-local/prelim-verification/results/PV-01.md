The active design is not ready for implementation. The concept, specification, and guide do not yet describe one input contract. The disk checker cannot justify Step 0. PV-01 is complete as a static audit, but its proposed replacements remain unproven until their fixtures run.

Revise after evidence. Preserve every existing requirement ID. Do not start V5 Step 0. Do not assign successor IDs before the relevant fixture states the replacement claim.
