## Inference

The generator produces the interface that PV-13 proved on Nix. The checks that mattered most were structural: Nix read the input names, `PATH` held no Vendomat command, and the two owned files were unreadable during `sync`. A text search could not have passed `GEN-016`, because the header names the tool.

The registry sources used `path:` and local `git+file` URLs. Those are stand-ins for portable remote URLs. They do not prove that a fleet host can fetch a private source (PV-15).

## Decision

Record `GEN-015` to `GEN-022` and `REG-011` to `REG-015` as fixture-passed. Keep them out of fleet acceptance. `DEL-010` (the host CLI in an existing shell) and `DEL-011` have no fixture yet. `vendomat add` and `vendomat remove` do not exist, so `REG-007` and `REG-009` are checked only for `sync`.
