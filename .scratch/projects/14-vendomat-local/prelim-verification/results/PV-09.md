## Inference

Attic can serve this closure to a cold Nix store when the caller already has the pull credential and uses the host-local route. This does not prove installer access, first-boot access, or a cold laptop download. The current Serve URL and `attic use` output do not agree on the route prefix. The host lacks the private cache trust key and credential in its Nix configuration.

## Decision

Block CACHE-007 and BOOT-019 until an installer route, trust key, pull credential, source flake, and cold VM or image fixture are specified and tested. Keep the partial host-local cold-store result. Do not claim a laptop install downloads from Attic. Keep production cache settings unchanged.

## Follow-up

Rework the bootstrap design to make the installer able to reach the cache or receive a verified closure before first boot. Then repeat the transfer in a disposable VM with a cold store. Keep the actual private credential out of tracked files and logs.
