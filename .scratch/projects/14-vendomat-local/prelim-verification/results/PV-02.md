## Inference

The stable IDs, model, serial, exact size, UUID ownership, and root/boot ancestry match the recorded inventory on this host. `lsblk` reports no target partitions or mounts. These observations do not prove the target has no partition-table or filesystem signature because `wipefs --no-act` could not read it.

The synthetic checker rejects the requested unsafe cases and fails closed on unknown signature data. It is not an implementation or authorization to write to a physical disk. The existing guide's Step 0 remains withdrawn and its `PREFLIGHT PASS` claim is unsupported.
